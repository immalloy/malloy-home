Your Little Oyachi is a small, calming pet game where you can pet and spend relaxing moments with Oyachi.

This is one of my first and only games, built in **PixiJS** and ported to Desktop with **NeutralinoJS**.

## About the game

Originally created as a small anniversary gift for my Girlfriend (the love of my life), this game is all about simplicity and love :D
